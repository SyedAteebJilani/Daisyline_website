About DaisyLine Crochet

Every loop, knot, and stitch is a tribute to patient craftsmanship. DaisyLine creates handmade crochet pieces with care, creativity, and attention to detail.

Our Story

DaisyLine Crochet began from a passion for handmade crochet and creating meaningful pieces that feel personal.

In a world dominated by mass production, we take pride in slow-made, handcrafted textile art that carries warmth, love, and timeless charm.

Custom Order Process

Step 1 — Talk to Us

Contact DaisyLine through WhatsApp or social media to discuss the idea.

Step 2 — Finalize Design & Price

The designer discusses the requirements and provides the final quote.

Step 3 — Advance Payment

Once the design and price are approved, pay the required advance.

Step 4 — Upload Payment Screenshot

The payment screenshot is required when submitting the Custom Order form.

Step 5 — Submit Custom Order Form

Complete the Custom Order form with your requirements. Reference images can be uploaded optionally.

Policies & Information

  • Delivery available across Pakistan.
  • Handmade products require processing time.
  • Small/simple orders generally require around 5–10 days after confirmation.
  • Complicated orders may require additional time.
  • Customers can contact DaisyLine if they need clarification.
  • Advance payment screenshot is required before the order is confirmed.
  • Confirmation email is received once the order is confirmed.
  • Custom products made according to the customer's requirements are not returnable.
  • Ready-to-ship products are returnable only when there is a genuine size issue.
  • Other reasons are not eligible for return/refund.
  • Customers must contact DaisyLine within 2–4 days if they need to report an eligible issue.
  • Return policy applies only to ready-to-ship items.
