About DaisyLine Crochet

🌸

Our Story & Craft Philosophy

DaisyLine Crochet began with a passion for slow living and timeless textile art. In a world dominated by mass production, we create slow-made, handcrafted crochet pieces that tell a story. From floral Gajras to soft plushies and cozy apparel, every item is individually crocheted using premium hypoallergenic yarns.

🛍️

How to Order

Ordering from DaisyLine is easy! Browse our ready-to-ship shop catalog or request a custom order by clicking "Custom Orders" in the navigation. Select your preferred color swatches, complete your details, and we'll handle the rest.

🚚

Shipping Policy

We deliver nationwide across Pakistan! Standard courier delivery takes 2–4 business days after your order is hand-crafted. Free shipping is available on orders over PKR 3,000.

🔄

Return & Exchange Policy

Because each item is custom crafted by hand, all sales are final. However, if your item arrives damaged or incomplete, please contact our support team on WhatsApp within 24 hours for a replacement.

❓

Frequently Asked Questions

For complete details on yarn care, custom order timelines, and payment options, please visit our dedicated FAQs Page.
