The Anatomy of a Daisyline Cardigan.

True luxury isn't found in a factory line. It is woven stitch by stitch, loop by loop, over countless hours. We source only the finest, softest premium yarns to ensure that your cardigan isn't just a beautiful statement piece, but a durable layer designed to last a lifetime.

Premium Fibers

We use highly durable, color-fast cotton and soft velvet yarns.

Slow Made Process

Each cardigan takes over a week to complete, ensuring absolute perfection in tension and fit.

Heritage Inspired
